# Enterprise AI Governance Framework

A comprehensive governance framework for organizations adopting Artificial Intelligence at scale.

## Objective

Enable safe, secure, responsible, compliant, and business-aligned AI adoption while maximizing enterprise value.

## Governance Domains

- AI Strategy
- AI Risk Management
- AI Security
- Responsible AI
- Data Governance
- Privacy & Compliance
- Model Governance
- Agentic AI Governance
- FinOps & Cost Management
- Monitoring & Auditability

## Governance Pillars

1. Strategic Alignment
2. Responsible AI
3. Security by Design
4. Compliance by Design
5. Human Oversight
6. Auditability
7. Risk Management
8. Cost Optimization
